# Accessibility and interface audit (SE-071)

Checklist of DESIGN §8, with the evidence for each item. Every test named here runs in the
E2E suite (`npx playwright test <file>`) or in `npm run check` (unit tests); the browser is
Chrome (Chrome for Testing 156 in the run-4 container, SwiftShader WebGPU, `CI=1`).

How the audit finds what to check: the dialogs, popovers, menus, panels and tools are read
from the app's registries at run time (`tests/e2e/se-071-support.ts`, `visitAllSurfaces`):
every command of the command registry whose title ends in "…" is run and the surface it
opens is visited — the run fails if one of them is disabled there or opens neither a surface
nor a picker, and Layer Properties… is visited on a pixel layer and on a reference layer
(its Placement) — the four dialogs that answer a situation rather than such a command (the
discard question, the overwrite question, the drop chooser, recovery at launch) are brought
about; the run then fails unless every dialog kind of the dialog registry
(`ui/dialogs/registry.ts`) and every app surface (`app/surfaces/surfaces.ts`) was visited.
Popovers, which have no kind, are recorded by name.
Menus come from `MENU_BAR` and the registry's menu paths, context menus from the names the
commands list in `contextMenus`, dock panels from the dock registry, context bars from the
tool registry.

## Side-by-side review against DESIGN.md and the mockup

Read from the code and stylesheets, and from screenshots of the running app (dark and light,
1280 × 720 and 1536 × 864) beside `docs/design/mockup.html`. Fixed:

- The timeline's splitter took the presses on the top edge of the transport buttons, and the
  dock's splitter overlapped it at the corner (found by the layout check). The timeline's
  content now lies above the splitter's hit area inside the panel (`z-index` on
  `.shell-timeline__content`: the timeline's scroller has `contain: strict`, a stacking context
  of its own, so a `z-index` on the transport bar inside it could not lift it over the
  splitter); the splitter keeps its line and the 4 px above the border.
- The tab strip's "All documents" button had no tooltip of the app's own.
- The shown variant in a tool slot's menu had its name in `--accent` on the highlighted row's
  `--bg-active`: 3.9:1 in the light theme (axe `color-contrast`, found by
  `se-071-axe-shell.spec.ts`). Now only its icon is accent, as on the active tool button
  (DESIGN §4); the name is `--text`.
- Ad-hoc spacing (above).

Recorded in DESIGN.md as deliberate: the 2 px and 3 px radii of small marks inside controls
(§3.3), the splitters' hit areas beside controls (§4, Splitter), the layer name's inset on the
spacing scale where the mockup has 6 px (§5.6), the one on/off idiom (§4, Toggle).

Seen and left as DESIGN describes them: unpressed text toggles show only their name (the
mockup's `.toggle`); the tool rail has two columns at 1280 × 720 (§5.1); the Palette section
is collapsed under 800 px of window height (§2).

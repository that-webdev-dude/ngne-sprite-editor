# Reference measurements for the move onto `@ngne/editor-ui` 0.1.0: 2026-11-26

The owner's run on branch `feat/editor-ui-migration` (`8dc6514` with the migration's
uncommitted changes, issue #21): the three benchmarks of the rows the migration can move, on
the final code, alone: no other benchmark or E2E ran at the same time. Records written
21:14–21:17 UTC.

Environment: Windows 11 10.0.26200, i7-12650H (16 logical processors), 16 GB RAM; installed
Chrome 158.0.8127.38 (new headless), Node 24.15.0. Intel UHD (gen-12lp) and RTX 4060 Laptop
GPU (nvidia/lovelace); every benchmark verified the requested WebGPU adapter. These are
reference-hardware measurements, not SwiftShader.

Method: the unchanged [benchmark scripts](../../tests/perf/README.md), one process at a time,
`PERF_RECORD=1`, `PERF_GPU=intel-uhd` / `rtx-4060`, no `PERF_ID`: each record replaces the
benchmark's own record. The other records stay those of [2026-11-21](2026-11-21.md).

## Notes

- **Upload frame switch.** Both sweeps now lie at 12.1–12.4 ms p95 on both GPUs. The RTX 4060's
  first sweep, 22.7 ms at 1.1.1, is 12.2 ms. The re-trial's report-only first sweep on Intel UHD
  (30.6 ms, on a busy machine) does not recur.
- **Cold load, RTX 4060.** One load of the ten took 1322 ms, 1303 ms of it to the shell, while
  its DOMContentLoaded came at 123 ms. The other nine took 531–888 ms. The warm-up took 2922 ms,
  2533 ms of it page to adapter (1042 / 772 ms at 1.1.1). The median is 52 ms lower than at
  1.1.1.
- **Cold load, Intel UHD.** The loads took 241–264 ms.
- **Create → first viewport frame** (details, not the result). Intel UHD median 250 ms, against
  255 ms at 1.1.1. RTX 4060 median 381 ms, largest 604 ms, against 278 ms at 1.1.1: about
  100 ms more in every load. Chrome changed from 157 to 158 between the two runs.
